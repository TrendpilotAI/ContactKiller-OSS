# Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ContactKiller community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Make room for newcomers and explain project-specific terminology.
- Respect privacy and never expose another person's contact or relationship data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing real contact data, credentials, private messages, or customer information in community spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through this repository's private **Report a vulnerability** flow and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith, document the relevant behavior, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.
